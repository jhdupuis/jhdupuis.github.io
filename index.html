The purpose of product marketing is to align a company’s value and capabilities with market and customer needs. Good product marketing influences every stage of the customer journey, from awareness and consideration to evaluation and onboarding.

To get there, product marketing teams need deep knowledge of customers, marketing, competitors, and the product, along with cross-functional relationships that help them take ownership of the team’s messages.

I love Emma Stratton’s work on punchy messaging. Great messaging and positioning starts with VBF.

  • V: Value proposition — kick things off with a big goal that resonates.
  • B: Benefits — show off what your product provides.
  • F: Features — explain how the product achieves those benefits.
